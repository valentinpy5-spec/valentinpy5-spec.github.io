Aerospace engineering student at [ISAE-SUPAERO](https://www.isae-supaero.fr/), I'm deeply interested about numerical methods applied to fluid dynamics and high-performance computing (HPC). My core interest lies at the intersection of theoretical CFD (turbulence modelling, combustion) and numerical scheme development (FDM/FVM/LBM/Galerkin methods).

Starting September 2026, I will follow the **Master 2 Recherche et Innovation (M2RI)** at Université Toulouse III - Paul Sabatier, co-accredited with ISAE-SUPAERO, deepening my background in rigorous numerical analysis of PDEs (scalar conservation laws, controlled dynamical systems, ill-posed inverse problems) alongside high-performance computing (MPI/OpenMP/CUDA).

I am actively looking for **master internship opportunities** focused on applied mathematics - fluid dynamics and numerical methods. This page gathers a selection of the personal and academic projects I have built along the way, feel free to explore the [projects](/al-folio/projects/) page, or check out my full [CV](/al-folio/cv/).
